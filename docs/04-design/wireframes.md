## Mobile

- Brand and headline stack above the chat panel.  
- Chat panel becomes full-width; quick replies wrap.  
- Ops table scrolls horizontally.

## Implemented mapping

The production UI follows this wireframe with teal brand colour, Sora/Manrope typography, and a full-bleed atmospheric background rather than card-heavy chrome.
