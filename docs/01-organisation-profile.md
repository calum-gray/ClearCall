# Organisation profile — ClearCall Communications Ltd

**Trading name:** ClearCall  
**Legal name:** ClearCall Communications Ltd  
**Sector:** UK telecoms (mobile, fibre broadband, landline/VoIP, business)  
**HQ:** Manchester, UK  
**Founded:** 2011 (fictional)  
**Customers:** ~4.2 million (fictional figure for the assignment)

## What the company does

ClearCall sells mobile plans, fibre broadband, landline/VoIP, and some business connectivity. They also have a few high-street / mall stores.

## Mission (short)

Keep customers connected and make support easier to get hold of.

## The problem we were asked to fix

Support teams are getting more of the same questions: “what’s my balance?”, “why is my bill high?”, “is there an outage?”, “I need a new SIM”, and so on. Queues are longer, customers wait more, and it costs more to run the contact centre. Management asked our software team to build a chatbot that can deal with the common stuff and pass people to a human when needed.

## Our role

We are the software development team for this assignment. We gathered requirements, designed the system, built Clara, tested it, and prepared the demo/poster.
